Talk to Pointr on any website

Pointr shows you where to click, one step at a time. You can type what you want to do, or just say it.

Allow the microphone once here. Websites never get your microphone; only Pointr does, and only while you are talking to it.

All set! Press Alt+X on any website to talk to Pointr.

Press it again when you finish talking. You can also click the microphone in Pointr's box.

!

The microphone is blocked for Pointr.

Open the settings, set Microphone to Allow, then come back and press the button again.

You can close this tab when you are done. Voice is turned into text on this computer.
